# IEEE Citation Style – Extended Examples for JRC

## Journal Articles (Primary Format – 100% target)

### Standard Journal Article
```
[1] A. B. Author and C. D. Author, "Title of the article in sentence case," 
    IEEE Transactions on Control Systems Technology, vol. 31, no. 2, 
    pp. 450–465, Mar. 2023, doi: 10.1109/TCST.2022.3189234.
```

### Article with Many Authors (6+, use "et al." after 6th)
```
[2] A. Author, B. Author, C. Author, D. Author, E. Author, F. Author et al., 
    "Title of article," Robotics and Autonomous Systems, vol. 158, 
    p. 104275, Dec. 2022, doi: 10.1016/j.robot.2022.104275.
```

### Article with DOI
```
[3] M. A. Johnson and R. K. Singh, "Adaptive fuzzy PID controller for 
    robotic arm trajectory tracking," Journal of Robotics and Control, 
    vol. 4, no. 3, pp. 312–325, Jun. 2023, doi: 10.18196/jrc.v4i3.18742.
```

## Conference Papers (Use Sparingly in JRC)

```
[5] A. B. Smith, "Title of conference paper," in Proc. IEEE Int. Conf. 
    Robotics and Automation (ICRA), London, U.K., May 2023, pp. 1234–1239,
    doi: 10.XXXX/XXXXX.
```

## Books and Book Chapters

### Book
```
[6] R. C. Dorf and R. H. Bishop, Modern Control Systems, 13th ed. 
    Hoboken, NJ, USA: Pearson, 2022.
```

### Book Chapter
```
[7] A. Author, "Chapter title," in Book Title, B. Editor, Ed. 
    City, Country: Publisher, Year, pp. X–Y.
```

---

## Thesis / Dissertation (avoid if possible)
```
[8] A. B. Author, "Title of dissertation," Ph.D. dissertation, 
    Dept. Elect. Eng., Univ. Name, City, Country, Year.
```

## Practical Notes for JRC Authors

1. **DOI is MANDATORY** — every reference must end with `doi: 10.XXXX/XXXXX`. If a paper has no DOI, replace it with a properly indexed alternative. Verify all DOIs at https://doi.org
2. **Scopus/WoS indexing required** — minimum 80% of references must come from Scopus or WoS indexed journals. Check journal indexing at https://www.scopus.com/sources
3. **Include 1–2 JRC citations** — search http://journal.umy.ac.id/index.php/jrc for relevant papers
4. **Self-citation cap: 10%** — with 60 refs, maximum 6 can be your own prior work
5. **Always include DOI** when available — reviewers check this
6. **Abbreviate journal names** per IEEE standard (e.g., "IEEE Trans. Ind. Electron." not the full name) — OR use the full name consistently
7. **Month abbreviations**: Jan., Feb., Mar., Apr., May, Jun., Jul., Aug., Sep., Oct., Nov., Dec.
8. **Volume and number**: use "vol." and "no." 
9. **Page range**: use en-dash (–) not hyphen (-): pp. 123–145
10. **In-text citation**: use bracketed numbers [1], [2]–[5], [1], [3], [7]
11. **Citation order**: numbered in order of appearance in text

## Checking Your References Meet JRC Requirements

Run through this for EACH reference:
- [ ] Is it a journal paper? (not website, report, or mostly conference)
- [ ] Is it published within the last 5 years? (2021–2026 for 2026 submission)
- [ ] Is it indexed in Scopus or WoS? (verify at scopus.com or webofscience.com)
- [ ] Does it have a valid DOI? (mandatory — verify at doi.org)
- [ ] Is the IEEE format correct? (Author, "Title," Journal, vol, no, pp, year, doi)
- [ ] Is it cited in the text?

Run through this for the ENTIRE reference list:
- [ ] Total count ≥ 60?
- [ ] Scopus/WoS indexed references ≥ 80%?
- [ ] At least 1–2 references from JRC (Journal of Robotics and Control)?
- [ ] Self-citations ≤ 10% of total (max 6 out of 60)?
- [ ] All entries have DOI?
